I got a call for an injured Great egret in a small covered emptied fish pool.
Getting there I noticed it would be hard to run with my sandlas and my long pents.
I took the sandlas off and stayed bare foot - but the pents still made it hard to run.
Anyhow, I chased it in the pool and was able to catch it, not before it tried to take my eye out.
As a "bonus", not reported in the original call, in the same pool there was an injured Pygmy cormorant, see below for its own full story.
