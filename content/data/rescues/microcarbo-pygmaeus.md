The Pygmy cormorants are hard to catch, and rarely are.
The reason is that they know how to dive, and I don't - not as good as they are.
This one was found in the same pool as an injured Great egert, and since the pool was empty, I thought it would be an easy catch.
But it found its way out of the pool and jumped into a small pool near it. A real small one luckly for it and me.
I went after it and with a patient of a hunter I let it feel it is unsafe to stay in the water - it went out.
But when I chased it it went back into the safty of the water. A few turns of this game convined it that it is better to run away to another place.
This was my time to actually rescue it, but of course, I got bytes from its sharp beak before securing it.
