The birds of Israel, with the Latin names broken into their meaningful parts.

**Content:** A complete list of the birds recorded in Israel, organized taxonomically from family to species — 565 species · 84 families.

**Details:** The data is kept in three languages — Hebrew, English and Latin (scientific). Each Latin name is also broken into its meaningful components, with the meaning of every part.

**Outputs:** The same data drives two outputs: a printable book and an interactive learning aid.

**Book:** An A5 PDF of about 100 pages, [available for download](assets/documents/israel_birds_one_file.pdf). Its core is the _complete list_; an extensive set of indexes lets the reader find an entry from almost any part of a name — the Hebrew name, the English name, the Latin name, the family, or a name component. The book also explains the Latin components, making the scientific names easier to understand.

**Learning Aid:** An interactive, personal, success-driven, web-based tool for memorizing the list. It is built on the same data and gradually builds a thorough knowledge of the material. Once the list is firmly memorized with the aid, the book is rarely opened.
