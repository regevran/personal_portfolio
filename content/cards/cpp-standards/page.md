Beyond authoring and co-authoring the papers below, I take part in WG21 meetings, **in person and remotely**.

Standardizing C++ requires understanding the language and how it is specified, but also the ability to <mark>listen, learn, reject and come up with other ideas</mark> in the face of extremely talented experts.

I find it interesting, challenging and rewarding. _I know most people don't understand why._
