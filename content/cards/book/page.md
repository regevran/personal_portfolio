I designed and edited this book to be caried to the field if one wants to.
It is now in its third edition as editing a book is not an easy task at all.
I switch edition after physically printing the book and learning its flaws.
The next edition will have fixes to content, up till now it had mostly just fixes to structure.
The book lists 565 species in 84 families. Each entry sets the Hebrew name beside English and Latin names.
The book unpacks the Latin name into the words it was built from. 
Indexes at the end of the book by Hebrew name, English name, Latin name, family, and name component make it possible to enter the book from almost any part of a bird name.
It is a learning aid rather than a field guide.
