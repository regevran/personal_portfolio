Third edition · 2026
:::

I designed and edited this book to be carried into the field. It is now in its **third edition**. Editing a book is not an easy task at all.

I switch editions after physically printing each one and learning its flaws. The next edition will have fixes to **content**; up till now, most changes have been to **structure**.

The book lists **565** species in **84** families. Each entry sets the *Hebrew* name beside the *English* and *Latin* names, then <mark>unpacks the Latin</mark> name into the words it was built from. **Five indexes** — *Hebrew* name, *English* name, *Latin* name, family, and name component — make it possible to enter the book from almost any part of a bird name.
